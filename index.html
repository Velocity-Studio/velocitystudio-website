After submittal, the city reviews. State law gives them 60 days on an ADU, and most take all of it.

What is included

One stamped set. Every department that has to sign off.

Who reviews it

  • Building
  • Planning and zoning
  • Fire
  • Public Works
  • Grading
  • Health
  • Urban forestry
Which of these review your project depends on your city and your lot. We find out before the call.

What they get

  • Cover, general notes, code summary
  • Site plan
  • Floor plans and roof plan
  • Elevations and building sections
  • Door, window, and finish schedules
  • Structural plans, details, calculations
  • Plumbing, electrical, mechanical
  • Title 24 energy report and CALGreen
  • Architect's stamp; engineer's stamp when required
  • Plan check responses until the permit issues

Not included: survey, soils report, civil grading plan, fire sprinkler design, will-serve letters, and city fees. You pay those providers directly, and we tell you on the call if your lot needs any of them.

What those usually cost
  • Boundary or topographic survey: usually $1,500 to $4,000. A sloped lot needs one before Day 1.
  • Soils report: rare for an ADU; required on some hillside lots.
  • Civil grading and drainage plan: when the city asks for one.
  • Fire sprinkler design: a deferred submittal by the installer.
  • Utility will-serve letters, city plan check, impact, and school fees: paid to the city and utilities.
  • Stock plan license, if you bring a plan from a plan website.

Straight answers

Six things people ask before they book.

Does 5 days include the city's review?

No. You get a submittable set in 5 business days. The city then has up to 60 days on an ADU. We answer their comments within 3 business days until the permit issues, at no extra cost.

Is the price the whole price?

For the drawing set, yes. You pay the city's fees directly, and a survey or soils report if your lot needs one. We tell you on the call.

Why not my city's free pre-approved ADU plans?

Use them if one fits. Most people find the size or shape is wrong, or that the plan still needs a site plan, an energy report, structural work, and someone to carry it through plan check. We do all of that in the same five days.

Can I change things?

Yes: live on Day 1, then one written list. After the plan is locked, changes are quoted per change, because each one moves the delivery date.

What if my project turns out not to be simple?

We find out before you pay. If your city needs a hearing first or your lot has a problem, we say so on the call and quote it the regular way, or tell you who else to talk to.

Do you build it, or find me a contractor?

No. We draw and engineer for permit. If you want a bid package your contractors can price from the same set, ask on the call.
