Permits are usually painful

Most architects deliver permit sets 3-5x slower than they should.

Their tools are old, their teams are thin, their process is loose, there is no urgency, and your project shares a queue with a dozen others that stop and start.

  • Time. You wait days for each reply, weeks for each round of drawings, and months for a permit set.
  • Money. Architecture, engineering, and energy work are billed separately, revisions add up, and if you are carrying a loan on the property, every extra month costs you money.
  • Your sanity. Most architects are unresponsive and manage projects poorly, so the ball ends up in your court and you spend the project chasing them for the next step.

Straight answers

What people ask before they book.

Does the timeline include the city's review?

No. You get a submittable set in 5 to 10 business days, by project size. The city then has up to 60 days on an ADU. We answer their comments within 3 business days until the permit issues, at no extra cost.

Is the price the whole price?

For the drawing set, yes. You pay the city's fees directly, and a survey or soils report if your lot needs one. We tell you on the call.

Do I have to pick from your designs?

No. Bring a plan you bought, a house you like, a sketch, or nothing. We sketch it with you on the first call. Templates are there if you want a head start.

Can I change things?

Yes: live on the first call, then one round of revisions within 24 hours. After the design is locked, changes are quoted per change, because each one moves the delivery date.

What if my project turns out not to be simple?

We find out before you pay. If your city needs a hearing first or your lot has a problem, we say so on the call and quote it the regular way, or tell you who else to talk to.

Why not my city's free pre-approved ADU plans?

Use them if one fits. Most people find the size or shape is wrong, or that it still needs a site plan, energy report, structural work, and someone to carry it through plan check. We do all of that in the same five days.

Do you build it, or find me a contractor?

No. We draw and engineer for permit. If you want a bid package your contractors can price from the same set, ask on the call.
