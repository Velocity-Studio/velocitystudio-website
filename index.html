What you get

Everything the city needs to issue your permit.

In the set

  • Cover sheet, general notes, and code summary
  • Site plan
  • Floor plan and roof plan
  • Exterior elevations and building sections
  • Door, window, and finish schedules
  • Structural plans, details, and calculations
  • Plumbing, electrical, and mechanical plan sheets
  • Title 24 energy report and CALGreen checklist
  • Architect's stamp on every set, structural engineer's stamp when the city requires it
  • Responses to plan check comments until the permit issues
  • A PDF package sized and named for your city's portal

Not in the set

  • Boundary or topographic survey (we tell you if you need one; usually $1,500 to $4,000)
  • Soils report (rare for an ADU; required on some hillside lots)
  • Civil grading and drainage plan
  • Fire sprinkler design (deferred submittal by the installer)
  • Utility will-serve letters
  • City plan check, impact, and school fees
  • Stock plan license if you bring a plan from a plan website

Add-ons

  • We file the application in our name and manage the portal for you: $2,500
  • Design changes you ask for after sign-off: quoted per change before we start

If your project needs an approval before the building permit

Some projects need a hearing or a review board before the city will accept a building permit application: hillside development review, a coastal development permit, design review, an HOA architectural committee, or a historic certificate of appropriateness. We handle those, but they are a different kind of work. We scope the agency, the submittal, and the meetings first, then quote it, usually $8,000 or more per approval. Once the approval is in hand, your permit set goes through the fast track like everyone else's. We check your parcel before our call and tell you if this applies to you.

How it works, day by day

Five business days for an ADU. Ten for a home or a renovation.

The schedule works because nothing is invented on your job. You start from a layout we have already built and engineered, you tell us how to change it, and the team does the rest at once instead of one discipline after another.

Before the clock starts

Book a 15-minute call. We research your parcel before it, so on the call we can tell you which track you are on and the exact price. Then you finish intake (address, photos, your starting layout, bedrooms and bathrooms, a few preferences) and pay the 50% deposit. Day 1 is the next business day.

Is it a fit

Fast track works for most lots in California. Not all.

Works when

  • You own the property or are in contract on it
  • You want to submit to the city within the next month
  • Your project is one of the three types above
  • Your city accepts the building permit application directly, with no hearing first
  • You can make decisions in one sitting and give us one list of changes

Does not work when

  • A board, commission, or HOA has to approve the design before the city will take the application
  • You want a design from scratch with open-ended revisions
  • You need someone to build it, not just draw it
  • The project is outside California

Steep lots, fire zones, and corner lots are fine. A sloped lot needs a topographic survey before Day 1, and we will tell you if yours does.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they book.

How can you do this in 5 days when everyone else quotes 4 to 8 weeks?

Because we do not start from a blank sheet. The ADU layouts are already drawn and engineered. Your session changes them to fit you and your lot. Structural, energy, and MEP are done at the same time by different people instead of one after another. And we gather everything before the clock starts, so no one is waiting on a photo on Day 3.

Does 5 days include the city's review?

No. We deliver a set ready to submit in 5 business days. The city then reviews it. State law gives them 60 days on an ADU. When they send comments, we answer within 3 business days and keep answering until the permit issues, at no extra cost, as long as the comments are about the set and not about a change you asked for after submittal.

Why pay $6,000 when my city has free pre-approved ADU plans?

Use them if one fits. Most people find the pre-approved plan is the wrong size, the wrong shape for their lot, or still needs a site plan, an energy report, structural adaptation, and someone to carry it through plan check. We start from a layout that fits you and finish all of that in the same five days.

I saw ADU plans online for $4,000. Why are you more?

Those are usually drafting only, with no engineer, no energy report, and a 4 to 6 week wait. Ours is the complete stamped set in a week. If the cheaper option works for your timeline, take it.

Is the price the whole price?

For the drawing set, yes. You will pay the city's fees directly. If your lot needs a survey or a soils report, you pay that provider directly, and we tell you on the call whether you need one.

Who draws my plans?

A project architect on our team, with me reviewing and stamping. You meet them on Day 1 and they are the person who emails you every day.

Can I change things?

Yes. The Day 1 session is where most of that happens, live, on a shared screen. Then you send one written list of changes and we lock the plan. After that, changes are quoted per change, because every change after lock moves the delivery date.

What if my project turns out not to be simple?

We find that out before you pay. We research your parcel before the call. If your city requires a hearing first, or your lot has a problem that puts it off the fast track, we say so on the call and quote it the regular way, or tell you who else to talk to.

What do I need to have ready?

Your address, photos of the lot and any existing building, which layout you like, how many bedrooms and bathrooms, and your deposit. For renovations, the Hover photos, the room scans, and the walkthrough video. Most people finish in under an hour.

Do you build it or find me a contractor?

No. We draw and engineer for permit. If you want a bid package your contractors can price from the same set, ask on the call.

Do you work outside California?

Not on the fast track. Our regular practice takes projects in other states.

Will it look like a box?

Look at the layouts. They are straightforward because that is what permits fast and builds cheap. Exterior finishes, roof form, windows, and trim are yours to choose in the session.
