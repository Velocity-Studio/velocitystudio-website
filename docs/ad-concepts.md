# Ad concepts for the fast-track homepage

Landing page: https://velocitystud.io (every link carries UTMs; the page passes them to Pipedrive and Calendly).
Audience: California homeowners 35 to 65 who already know they want an ADU, a remodel, or a simple home, and who have been quoted or ghosted by a slow drafter. Not developers, not plan aggregators.

## The structure every ad follows

1. **0 to 1.5 s, visual hook.** Something that catches the thumb before any copy: motion, a face, a stamped sheet.
2. **Pain, 1 to 3 s.** A question that names the viewer. "Still waiting on your drafter?"
3. **Solution, 1 to 3 s.** One line, standardized: **"Full permit sets for ADUs and homes under 3,000 sq ft, in one to two weeks."**
4. **Magnitude, 1 to 3 s.** The number: days, price, "stamped, engineered, Title 24 included."
5. **Proof, 1 to 3 s.** The drawings, the mechanism on real screens, or Richman's face and license. No luxury projects; they argue against "simple and fast."
6. **CTA, 1 to 2 s.** "Two minutes to see if your lot qualifies." URL.

Attention budget is 7 to 10 seconds; only V3 runs longer, because the face earns it.

Formats per concept: 4:5 (feed), 9:16 (Reels and Stories), 1:1. Keep text inside the center 80% of the frame.

- Primary text: "Most architects deliver permit sets three to five times slower than they should. We built a firm around not doing that. Here is how it works."

## I2 · 60 days versus 5 (image, text-led)
- Visual: forest green, two lines of large type, one sheet tile in a corner. "The city takes 60 days to review an ADU. We take 5 to draw it."
- Primary text: "State law gives the city 60 days on an ADU. Most drafters take longer than that just to hand you the set. We deliver a stamped, permit-ready set in 5 business days and answer every plan check comment within 3 business days until the permit issues."
- Headline: "Permit-ready in 5 business days."
- Assets: one sheet tile. Tests contrast claim against I1's direct claim.

## I3 · The sheet set (carousel, 5 cards)
- Cards: site plan, floor plan, elevations, framing, Title 24 and MEP, each a hero tile with a one-line caption; last card the cover-sheet graphic and the button. Headline: "Everything the city needs, in one stamped set."
- Primary text: "Cover and notes, site plan, floor and roof plans, elevations, sections, structural plans and calcs, plumbing, electrical, mechanical, Title 24. Drawn in parallel by one team and stamped by a California licensed architect. One to two weeks."
- For the buyer whose fear is "will the city accept it." Assets exist.

## I4 · The free pre-approved plan (image, ADU only)

- Primary text: "Pre-approved plans are free until you need a site plan, an energy report, structural adaptation, and someone to carry it through plan check. We start from a layout that fits your lot and finish all of that in the same five days."
- Headline: "Fits your lot. Permit-ready in 5 days."

## Measurement
- UTMs: utm_source=meta, utm_medium=paid, utm_campaign=fasttrack-w{n}, utm_content={concept}-{ratio}, e.g. V2-9x16.
- The form fires Meta "Lead" with the score; pass = tier high. Optimize toward the high tier through the CAPI deal-stage events, not raw submits.
- Judge on qualified leads per dollar, not clicks.

- Copy sheet: primary text, headline, description, CTA button, URL with UTMs, suggested ad set and audience.
