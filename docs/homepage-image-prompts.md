## Headshot: richman.jpg · 4:5

The reliable path is a real photo plus light AI cleanup, not an AI portrait from references. Reference-blended portraits drift in the small things people recognize (jaw line, eye spacing, hairline), which is where the uncanny feeling comes from. A real photo keeps your face; the model only fixes lighting and background.

### Shoot the reference set (15 minutes, a phone is fine)
- Stand facing a large window, daylight, no direct sun on your face, the window slightly to one side so one cheek is a touch brighter.
- Plain wall or a tidy office behind you, two steps back from it so it blurs. No ceiling lights on.
- Phone at eye level, portrait mode on, rear camera, someone else holding it or a tripod. Arms length is too close; stand about 2 m away and let them zoom in slightly.
- Wear what you would wear to a client call. Solid mid-tone colors; avoid fine patterns and pure white.
- Take 20 to 30 frames: chest-up, small changes in head angle and expression, including a relaxed closed-mouth smile and an open one. Pick the three best.

### Cleanup prompt (Gemini, with your chosen photo attached as the only reference)
Edit this photograph only. Keep the face, hair, expression, glasses, and proportions exactly as they are; do not change age, skin texture, or any facial feature. Replace the background with a softly out-of-focus, warm, neutral office interior with daylight from the left. Even out the lighting on the face to soft window light, remove the harsh shadow under the chin, and lightly reduce lens distortion. Crop to a 4:5 portrait from the chest up with the eyes about one third from the top. Natural color, no skin smoothing, no retouching of wrinkles. Output should look like a professional headshot taken by a photographer, not an illustration.

### If you still want a generated portrait
Attach three to five of the reference frames at once and use: "Create a professional headshot of the person in these reference photos. Match their face, age, hairline, eye color, and build exactly; do not idealize or make them younger. Chest-up, 4:5, looking at the camera with a relaxed closed-mouth smile, soft daylight from the left, a warm neutral blurred office background, a navy knit polo." Generate several, then compare each against the references at eye spacing, nose width, and jaw. Discard anything that looks "better" than the references; that is the drift.

The bio slot sits in the solution section on a white card. Warm neutral background and soft daylight match the generated scenes without any brand-color instruction. Target 1200 x 1500, JPG, about 200 KB.
