# Round three ad proposals (Nov 4)

Why Video 3 keeps winning, from the creative itself (17 s, three stock scenes, bold text cards):

| Seconds | Picture | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | Modern flat-roof house, topiary, lawn | DRAWINGS + ALL PERMITS INCLUDED, with a "Super-fast service" ticker |
| 4 to 9 | Older couple reading a plan set | ONE CONTRACT FOR ALL DISCIPLINES |
| 10 to 17 | Hands on floor plans | Apply Now button |

Body text: "Tired of waiting months just to break ground? Velocity Studio compresses the entire preconstruction stage into 3 days - from discovery to permit-ready drawings. To know more, chat for free." CTA: Contact Us.

What it does that ads A, B, I1 and I2 do not:
1. **It sells the bundle, not the price.** Three stacked promises (everything included, one contract, apply now) and no dollar figure. Our ads lead with $6,000 and "5 business days", which reads as an ADU offer to a homeowner and filters out anyone with a bigger job.
2. **The picture is a whole house.** Not a backyard ADU. People with a lot, a teardown, or a duplex see themselves in it. Five of the seven new-home leads came from this ad.
3. **Builder vocabulary.** "Break ground", "preconstruction", "all disciplines", "apply" are words a contractor or developer uses. Homeowners still respond, but pros self-select in.
4. **Motion with real people.** Stock footage of faces and hands outperforms our rendered stills for attention in feed.

The "3 days" line is wrong and should go. It lives in the body text, not in the video, so it is a copy fix, not a re-edit.

## Fix first: Video 3 body copy

Meta does not let a running ad's creative be edited, so this is a duplicate ad with new copy in the same ad set, old one paused once the new one has spend. Proposed body:

> Tired of waiting months just to break ground? Velocity Studio compresses preconstruction into days, not months: discovery to permit-ready drawings in 5 to 10 business days, with architecture, structural, MEP and Title 24 under one contract. California only. Chat for free.

Title: "Velocity Studio: Permit-ready in 5 to 10 business days". CTA stays Contact Us. Same video.
